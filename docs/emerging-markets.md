# Emerging Markets

## Why Stellar

Stellar was chosen for trustRent because it directly addresses the constraints of emerging market users:

- **$0.01/tx fees** — viable for weekly rent schedules and micro-payments
- **5-second settlement** — no waiting days for cross-border transfers
- **USDC native support** — protects against local currency volatility
- **Built-in DEX** — tenants can pay in local currency; landlords receive USDC
- **Anchor ecosystem** — on/off ramps via M-Pesa, mobile money, local banks

## Target Users

- Tenants in high-inflation economies paying rent in USDC to preserve value
- Landlords receiving cross-border rent from diaspora tenants
- Property agents automating commission collection
- Lenders and future landlords verifying rental history on-chain
